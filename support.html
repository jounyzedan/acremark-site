Frequently asked questions

How accurate is AcreMark?

The math is exact enough not to matter: area is computed geodesically on the curved Earth. In practice, accuracy depends on where the corners go.

  • Drawing: zoom in as far as you can before placing each corner, and place it on a visible feature such as a fence post, field edge or driveway corner. On a clear image, a few acres usually comes within 1–2%.
  • Satellite imagery can be a few meters off, or a few years old. Trees and shadows can hide the real edge.
  • Drag any corner to adjust it, and tap a side's length label to add a corner in the middle of that side.
How do I get the best GPS accuracy outdoors?
  • Before you start, wait a few seconds for the GPS dot in Walk mode. Green (±10 m or better) is ideal. Yellow (±10–25 m, common near buildings and trees) still records, and AcreMark smooths those readings. Red means the signal is too weak, and points are skipped until it improves.
  • Stay under open sky. Buildings, tall trees and steep valleys reduce accuracy.
  • Walk at a steady pace, hold the phone up rather than in a back pocket, and finish where you started.
  • AcreMark automatically skips fixes worse than ±25 m, weights the rest by their accuracy, and ignores the jitter from standing still. Use Pause when you detour around an obstacle.
  • If the app says Precise Location is off, turn it on in iOS Settings → AcreMark → Location. Without it, GPS is only accurate to about 1 km.
  • Phone GPS is typically good to ±3–5 m. On a 1-acre plot that's roughly ±3–6% of the area; on 10+ acres it's about ±1–2%. Walking the boundary twice and comparing is a good check.
Is this a legal survey? Does it show property lines?

No. AcreMark is for estimation only and is not a legal survey. It does not show property lines or parcel boundaries. For legal boundaries, deeds, disputes or construction, consult a licensed land surveyor.

How do I restore AcreMark Pro?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID that bought Pro. Pro also works for members of your Family Sharing group. Restoring is free, and you never need to buy Pro twice.

How do I export a measurement?

With Pro, open a measurement, tap Export in the bottom panel, then choose:

  • PDF report: a satellite snapshot, area in every unit, side lengths, coordinates, date and notes.
  • KML: opens in Google Earth.
  • GeoJSON: for GIS tools and geojson.io.

The iOS share sheet then lets you save the file to Files, AirDrop it, email it, and so on.

How do I move my plots to a new phone?

Go to Settings → Export backup file and save it to iCloud Drive. On the new phone, use Settings → Import backup file. Plots are merged, and nothing is overwritten with older data. Plots are stored only on your device, so delete the app only after you've made a backup.

Why does AcreMark ask for "Always" location access?

Only when you start a Pro walk recording, so recording can continue with the screen locked. If you decline, everything else still works, but recording pauses while the app is in the background.

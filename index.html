Land area, in acres, in seconds.

AcreMark measures fields, lots, lawns and fence lines on satellite maps, or by walking the boundary with GPS.

No account. No ads. One-time purchase.

Three ways to measure

  • Draw: tap the corners on satellite imagery, then drag to adjust, with unlimited undo.
  • Walk: record the perimeter with GPS as you walk or drive it. Weak fixes are filtered out automatically.
  • Distance: fence lines, frontage, driveways, any path.

Every unit

Acres, hectares, square feet, square meters, square yards and square miles, plus feet, meters, yards and miles. Area is computed geodesically on the curved Earth.

Honest pricing

Measuring is free and unlimited, and you can keep one saved plot. AcreMark Pro is a single in-app purchase. There's no subscription and no trial. Pro adds unlimited saved plots, PDF/KML/GeoJSON export, and walk recording that keeps going with the screen locked. Family Sharing is supported.

For estimation only — not a legal survey. AcreMark does not show property lines. Consult a licensed surveyor for legal boundaries.
